# Webtext — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Webtext is a Chrome extension that converts a portion of a webpage you select into Markdown and copies it to your clipboard.

## What Webtext does with your data

- **Page content.** When you click the Webtext toolbar icon and select elements, the extension reads the text, images, and video links inside those elements on that page only. This happens entirely on your device.
- **Clipboard.** The generated Markdown is written to your system clipboard so you can paste it wherever you like.
- **No collection.** Webtext does not transmit page content, clipboard content, browsing history, personal information, or usage data to the developer or to any third party. There is no analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
- **No storage.** Webtext does not save page content or your selections. Everything is discarded when the selection ends or the page is reloaded.
- **No remote code.** Webtext ships all of its code inside the extension package and does not load or execute remote scripts.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `activeTab` | Grants temporary access to the current tab only after you click the Webtext icon. |
| `scripting` | Injects the element picker into that tab, and only on that click. |
| `clipboardWrite` | Copies the generated Markdown to your clipboard. |

Webtext requests no host permissions and cannot run on `chrome://`, `edge://`, extension, or Web Store pages.

## Data sharing

Webtext shares no data with anyone. It has no servers and makes no network requests.

## Children's privacy

Webtext collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.
